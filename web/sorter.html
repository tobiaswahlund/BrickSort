The machine

The belt runs from left to right under the camera. As a piece crosses the middle of the camera's picture it's photographed and recognised; at the end of the belt a flap sends it to its bin. A piece the model isn't sure enough about goes to Rest, to be looked at by hand.

Log

    Everything here is simulated live in your browser: the pieces are LDraw 3D models dropped onto a moving belt with MuJoCo physics (as in BrickSort's desktop sorting machine), and the camera picture is drawn with three.js, so it's synthetic, not a photo. The sorting is real: the pieces are found in the camera picture, photographed as they cross the middle and recognised by the model, as on the machine. Only the score peeks at what each piece really is. It needs a computer with WebGL; phones may struggle.
