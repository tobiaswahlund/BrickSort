The machine

The belt runs left to right. Below the camera (dashed) each piece gets its answer; at the end a flap sends it to its bin. A piece the model isn't sure enough about goes to Rest, to be looked at by hand.

Log

    The pieces are pictures from BrickSort's 3D sorting machine (MuJoCo physics, LDraw parts), made on a PC: a browser can't run that, so here they slide along the belt as they landed. The recognising is real: the camera picture is cropped and the model guesses, as on the machine.
